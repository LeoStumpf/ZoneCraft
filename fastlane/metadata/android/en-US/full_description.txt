ZoneCraft builds map areas out of geometric rules. Each rule you add is
a zone — within a distance of a point, closer to one place than another,
above a chosen elevation — and where the zones overlap is the area that
satisfies all of them.

Useful wherever a place has to meet several conditions at once: planning
a move, scouting a site, teaching geography, plotting a walk, or any
"which parts of the map fit all of this?" question.

Want everywhere within 2 km of the station? Drop a circle. Everywhere
further out than that? Press Fill outside. Add a layer per rule, and the
overlap is your area.

SEVEN KINDS OF ZONE

• Circle — everything within a distance of a point, as a true geodesic
  radius rather than a shape drawn on a flat map.
• Nearest point — everywhere closer to one point than to any of the
  others. With two points it simply splits the map in half.
• Freehand line — draw a line to cut an area in two and keep one side.
• Freehand area — draw any shape by hand and fill it.
• Ground height — ground above or below an elevation you choose, built
  once from public terrain data and then kept offline.
• Places — import cafés, benches, drinking water, toilets or transit
  stations from OpenStreetMap, or name your own category and tap to place
  markers in it.
• Borders — download real district, city, state or country outlines
  once and keep them offline, coloured so no two neighbours match.

HOW THEY COMBINE

Overlapping zones merge into one clean colour instead of getting muddier.
Any layer can be inverted — "everywhere EXCEPT this" — and an adjustable
uncertainty band shows the fuzzy edge when your information is not exact.
Folders group layers so you can hide or invert a whole set at once, while
each layer keeps its own colour and settings.

ALSO

• Type a town, street or landmark and the map goes there.
• Every element can carry its own colour, so zones on one layer stay
  apart.
• Drag a shape's points on the map, or press Move and Save when it
  is right; every change can be undone.
• Import a city, river, park or boundary by name and edit it as your
  own shape.
• Import GeoJSON, KML, KMZ and GPX; export GeoJSON or KML — share a
  map with someone else, or bring in tracks from other apps.
• Measure elevation or distance anywhere on the map.
• "Locate me" centres the map on your position, only when you tap it.
• The map remembers where you left off, and tiles you have looked at
  keep working with no reception.

GIVING SOMETHING BACK

Spotted a bench in the wrong place, or one that is missing? Fix it, and
ZoneCraft can pass your change to OpenStreetMap as a note — only when you
press Send now, only with words you have read and edited, and never
automatically.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account. No sign-up. No ads. No tracking, analytics or crash
reporting. Everything you create stays in local storage on your device
and is never uploaded — there is no server to upload it to. It is not
backed up to Google either, so use Export to keep a copy. Location is
used only on your device, and only when you ask.
